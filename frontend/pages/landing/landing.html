About SkillLens AI

Your Personal AI Interview
Preparation Platform

SkillLens AI is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students and job seekers improve their interview performance through intelligent feedback, resume analysis, technical interview preparation, and performance tracking. The platform provides a structured learning journey from resume building to final interview readiness.

AI Resume Analysis HR Interview Practice Technical Interview Simulation Voice Interview Analysis AI Feedback Engine Performance Analytics Progress Tracking

Resume Intelligence

AI reads your resume like a recruiter would — scoring ATS compatibility, flagging missing skills, and surfacing concrete ways to strengthen it.

Interview Preparation

Practice HR and technical rounds in realistic simulated sessions, with every answer evaluated instantly by AI.

Performance Tracking

Every session feeds your analytics dashboard — see trends, strong and weak categories, and measurable improvement over time.

Career Growth

A structured journey from resume to offer — designed to build real, lasting interview confidence, not just quick tips.
